Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking with Urbania Luxury Tours Jaipur.

What is the price of Urbania Maharaja Seat rental in Jaipur?
▼
Urbania Maharaja Seat rental starts from ₹30 per km in Jaipur. Prices vary based on destination, trip duration, and route. Contact us on WhatsApp or call +91-8003243933 for a free personalised quote within 30 minutes.
Do you offer Chardham Yatra packages from Jaipur?
▼
Yes! We offer complete Chardham Yatra packages from Jaipur starting ₹24,999 per person. This covers Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath & Badrinath with comfortable Urbania transport, accommodation, and meals. Group and private packages both available.
What vehicles are available for Rajasthan tour packages?
▼
We offer Urbania Maharaja Seat (17 seater), Tempo Traveller (9–26 seater), Innova Crysta (7 seater), Ertiga (6 seater), Mini Bus (20–35 seater), and Luxury Coach Bus (40–45 seater). All vehicles are AC, GPS-tracked, and sanitized.
Is Urbania Luxury Tours available for corporate travel?
▼
Absolutely! We specialise in corporate tours, team outings, and employee travel across Rajasthan and North India. Our fleet of luxury buses handles groups from 10 to 500+ people with dedicated logistics coordination, invoicing, and GST billing.
What is the cancellation policy?
▼
We offer free cancellation up to 72 hours before your scheduled travel date. Cancellations within 72 hours are subject to a 25% charge. No-shows are charged fully. Contact us as early as possible for rescheduling — we always try to accommodate.
Do you arrange honeymoon packages in Rajasthan?
▼
Yes! Our honeymoon packages are specially curated for couples covering Udaipur, Jaisalmer, Jodhpur, and Mount Abu. Packages include luxury transport, heritage hotel stays, romantic dinners, rose-petal decorations, and personalised experiences.
